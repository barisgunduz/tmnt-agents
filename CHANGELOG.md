# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (2026-11-16)

### Added
- First release. Every agent is a pixel-art ninja turtle (Leonardo, Raphael,
  Donatello, Michelangelo) or Splinter, walking a strip at the bottom of the
  Herdr window.
- Working agents walk, idle ones stroll and fall asleep after 3 minutes,
  blocked ones stop and show a pulsing `!`, finished ones show a `✓`.
- Click a character to jump to its agent, right-click to choose who it is.
- `alt-shift-t` opens the picker for the focused agent.
- A `scale` prop resizes the characters.
- All art is generated by `tools/make_sprites.py` and ships in the mod. No
  downloads, no ImageMagick, no `curl`.
